# Contributing to bento

bento isn't accepting contributions until 2027.

bento is a fork of [niri](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri), developed separately.
Please report bento issues at https://github.com/faetalize/bento/issues, not to the niri project.

For questions, ideas and discussion, join the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/EZFTEvWWxC).

When reporting a bug, include the output of `bento -V` and, if you can, your config.
